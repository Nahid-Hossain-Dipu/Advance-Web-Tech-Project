# Many university students travel to campus every day, but public buses are crowded and ride-hailing is expensive. Some students look for shared rides in social-media groups, but these posts are hard to trust and do not show how many seats are left. EduCarpool is a web application where only verified members of one university can offer and join rides. A driver posts a trip with the pickup area, time, free seats, and a suggested cost share. A passenger searches for a trip, sends a seat request, and gets a notification when the driver replies. The system does not allow more passengers than seats, and phone numbers stay hidden until a request is accepted. Administrators verify users and handle reports. With this system, students can save money and travel time and ride with people they can trust.
